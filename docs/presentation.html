Problem Analysis & Motivation

The Crisis of Manual Investigation Management

Challenges of paper-bound Police Stations (Thanas) in Bangladesh

1. Broken Chain of Custody in Trials

Physical and digital items seized at crime scenes lack chronological tracking. If custodian handovers are unverified or timestamps are undocumented, evidence is routinely ruled inadmissible in Sessions Court under Section 9 of the Evidence Act.

Impact: Acquittals of major criminal syndicates on procedural technicalities.

2. Citizen Complaint Black Hole

Citizens filing grievances at Thanas receive handwritten slips with no digital audit trail. Complainants cannot verify whether an inquiry was launched, a GD recorded, or an FIR registered without repeated physical station visits.

Impact: Lack of transparency, public dissatisfaction, and delayed justice.

3. Information Silos Across Districts

Trans-regional syndicates operate seamlessly between Chattogram Port, Dhaka financial centers, and border crossings in Sylhet and Rajshahi. Disconnected station records prevent detectives from correlating repeat offenders and weapon ballistics.

Impact: Criminal rings exploit lack of unified relational intelligence.

4. The ORCUS Relational Solution

A unified 3NF schema integrating 5 Strategic Police Commands, atomic reference sequence numbering (`CMP`, `GD`, `FIR`, `CAS`), role-based access control, cryptographic SHA-256 evidence hashing, and an immutable audit trail.

Result: Zero data redundancy, seamless multi-officer collaboration, and judicial integrity.

Relational Database Theory

Relational Schema & 3NF Normalization

Elimination of redundancy, insertion, update, and deletion anomalies

Normalization Proof

1. First Normal Form (1NF): All attributes contain atomic values. Multi-valued contacts (phone, email) extracted into dedicated child entity `complainant_contact`.
2. Second Normal Form (2NF): All non-key attributes are fully functionally dependent on primary keys. In composite bridge tables (`case_suspect`, `case_victim`), operational roles like `role_in_crime` depend on the composite pair `(case_id, suspect_id)`.
3. Third Normal Form (3NF): No transitive dependencies ($X \to Y \to Z$). Officer badge numbers, branch details, and user credentials reside in distinct entities, preventing cascading corruption.
Total Tables: 28  |  Junction Bridges: 6  |  Audit Ledgers: 5

Referential Integrity & Constraints

  • `ON DELETE RESTRICT`: Safeguards critical historical records. A `case` cannot be deleted if active `evidence` exists; a `branch` cannot be dropped if assigned to active officers.
  • `ON DELETE CASCADE`: Applied cleanly to transient child lookups and bridge associations (e.g. removing a case automatically drops its `case_suspect` junctions).
  • Gapless System Sequences: Atomic generator table (`system_sequence`) prevents race conditions and ensures tamper-evident numbering: CMP-2026-0801 • GD-DHK-2026-0012 • FIR-2026-0101 • CAS-2026-0001

Forensic Integrity & Evidence Act

Tamper-Proof Evidence & Chain of Custody

Unbroken chronological provenance guaranteeing judicial court admissibility

The Custody State Progression

01
Seizure at Crime Scene: Packaging in anti-static or ballistic container; numbered seal applied (`SEAL-DHK-0091`).
02
Digital SHA-256 Hashing: Raw bitstream image hashed immediately. If even 1 bit flips during analysis, the hash differs.
03
Lab & Vault Transitions: Transfer logged in `evidence_status_history` with releasing officer, accepting officer, and reason.
04
Court Exhibition & Trial: Formally admitted into Metropolitan Sessions Court as physical Exhibit P-1.
